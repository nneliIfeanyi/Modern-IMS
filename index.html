How do I place an order?

Write your list in the order form, add any useful details, then choose “Send order to WhatsApp”. You can review the message before sending it.

Are prices confirmed before ordering?

Prices and availability should be confirmed by the store before you make a purchase. Sample products on this page do not show verified prices.

Can I request products not shown here?

Yes. Add the product name, preferred brand and quantity to your message, and ask the store to confirm whether it is available.

Can I choose pickup instead of delivery?

Yes, select your preferred option in the order form. The store can confirm the practical arrangements when they reply.
