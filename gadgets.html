How do I ask for a price?

Add the item and, if you know it, the preferred brand or model in the enquiry form. The message opens in WhatsApp so you can review it before sending.

Can I enquire about an item not shown?

Yes. Add its name and any useful details to your message and ask the store to check availability.

Is sending an enquiry a confirmed order?

No. Prices, availability and fulfilment should be confirmed by the store before a purchase is agreed.
